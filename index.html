How it works

  1. Play any song on YouTube or YouTube Music.
  2. Karaoke Key reads the song name from the video title.
  3. A small panel shows the key, major or minor, tempo and Camelot number, and stays visible in fullscreen.

Song key and tempo data provided by GetSongBPM.
